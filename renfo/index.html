Renfo is a companion app for renaissance festivals. Find your way around, see what’s on, and catch the joust before the crowd does.

Or use it in your browser ›

A personal project by Tyler Keegan · iPhone and web ·Join the beta

Festivals

Everything before you go.

Dates, hours, the weather and the address for each faire, with one tap to call, email or get directions.

Browse

Every faire, A to Z.

Pull up the sheet to search or scroll the full list of festivals, each with its own crest.

Countdown

What’s on, and what’s next.

Group festivals by what’s open now and what’s coming up, with a countdown in days to each one.

Badges

A badge for every faire.

Check in at a festival to earn its badge, and add a laurel for every year you go back.
