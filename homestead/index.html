A personal project by Tyler Keegan · In beta · iPhone & iPad
Requires an existing Home Assistant installation.

Controls

One tap. Done.

Adjust lights and climate, secure doors, run scenes, and control everyday devices with familiar Apple-platform interactions.

Areas

Explore your home.

Connect to your existing Home Assistant setup, with the devices, areas, and automations you already use. Browse devices and entities by area to find what you need.

History

Every detail tells a story.

Explore sensor history, spot trends, and see how your home changes throughout the day.

Customization

Every card. Your way.

Choose controls, adjust card sizes, and bring devices, charts, cameras, and weather together in dashboards that work for you.

Widgets

Your home, on your Home Screen.

Keep controls, device status, sensors, and actions close at hand with Home Screen widgets. Receive Home Assistant notifications to stay informed about your home.

Privacy

Private by design.

Homestead connects to the Home Assistant servers you choose. Credentials stay in your device’s Keychain. Homestead does not sell personal information or use third-party advertising. Read the privacy policy ›

Requires an existing Home Assistant installation. Homestead is an independent third-party client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Home Assistant project.
