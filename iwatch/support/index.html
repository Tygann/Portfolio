How can we help?

Have a question about iWatch or something that is not working? Email Tyler@Keegan.me with a brief description of what happened.

Reporting a problem

Include your iWatch version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help explain what you see.

TestFlight feedback

You can also send feedback through TestFlight. Open TestFlight, select iWatch, and choose Send Beta Feedback.

Protect your privacy

Do not send passwords or other private credentials. Hide personal information before sharing screenshots. Read the Privacy Policy for the current beta privacy information.
