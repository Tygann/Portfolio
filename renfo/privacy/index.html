Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Tyler Keegan handles information when you use Renfo’s app and websites, including renfo.app and web.renfo.app. Renfo helps you discover renaissance festivals, plan visits, and keep track of your favorites and festival badges.

Accounts and saved activity

If you create an account, Renfo handles your account identifier, email address, username or display name, and sign-in information. Sign-in is provided through Supabase, including email sign-in and Sign in with Apple where available. Apple may provide a private relay email address if you choose to hide your email.

Festival check-ins and earned badges are associated with your account so they can be available when you sign in. Favorites and other preferences may be stored locally on your device or in your browser. If you submit festival information, corrections, schedules, or images, Renfo stores the submission and its review history. Approved festival information may appear in the public directory.

Location

With your permission, Renfo uses your location for map and nearby-festival features and to verify festival check-ins. When you check in, the app sends your coordinates and related accuracy and timing information to Renfo’s API. The saved check-in record includes your account, festival, attendance year, distance from the festival, and validation method; it does not store your raw coordinates in that record.

You can turn off location access in your device or browser settings. Features that require your location, including verified check-ins, may then be unavailable.

Device storage and website analytics

The app and web client store preferences, cached content, and sign-in session information on your device or in your browser. The marketing website stores your appearance preference in browser local storage. Clearing website data or deleting the app can remove local information, but does not delete your account or server-stored activity.

Renfo uses Cloudflare for website and API hosting. The web app includes Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits and performance. Hosting and service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser or device details, request times, and diagnostic logs to deliver and protect the service.

Services and information sharing

Renfo uses Supabase for authentication and backend storage, Cloudflare for hosting, and Apple services for features such as maps and weather. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. Weather requests use festival coordinates. The web client also loads some libraries from third-party content delivery networks, which receive normal network request information.

Opening directions, festival websites, or other external links takes you to services with their own privacy policies. If you email support, the information you send is used to respond to your request. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Your choices and deletion

You can manage your account details and favorites in Renfo. The iPhone app provides a Delete Account option in the account screen. You can also email Tyler@Keegan.me to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. Account ownership may need to be verified before a request is completed.

Account information and saved activity are retained to provide the features you use until removed or your account is deleted. Support correspondence, security records, backups, and records required by law may remain for as long as needed for those purposes. Public festival information you contributed may remain in the directory after account deletion.

Children and security

Renfo is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it. We use safeguards to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Policy updates and contact

This page will be updated when Renfo’s privacy practices change, with the revision date shown above. Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to Tyler@Keegan.me.
