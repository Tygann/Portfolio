How can we help?

Have a question about Renfo or something that isn’t working? Email support@renfo.app. For a bug report, include your app version, device and operating system—or browser—and the steps that led to the issue.

Signing in

Use the same sign-in method you used to create your account. If you signed in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, your account may use an Apple private relay address. If you’re having trouble, check your internet connection and try again. Contact support if you still can’t access your account.

Location and check-ins

Renfo needs permission to use your location for nearby-festival features and verified check-ins. On iPhone, review Renfo’s location access in Settings. On the web, check your browser’s location permission for web.renfo.app.

If a check-in fails, make sure location access is enabled and try again while you’re at the festival. If it still doesn’t work, send the festival name and the error message. You don’t need to send your precise location.

Festival information

Spotted an incorrect date, address, or other detail? Send the festival name, what needs changing, and a link to the festival’s official information. Check the festival’s own website for the latest schedule before you travel.

Your account

You can manage account details from the account screen. To delete your account in the iPhone app, open that screen and choose Delete Account. If you need help accessing or deleting your account, email support. We may need to verify account ownership.

Protect your privacy

Don’t send passwords, sign-in codes, access tokens, or precise location information. If you include a screenshot, hide personal details first. Read the Privacy Policy for more about how Renfo handles information.
